# AI Digital Financial Twin for Saudi SMEs

## Overview

An AI-powered financial decision-support concept designed to help Saudi SMEs analyze their financial situation, forecast cash flow, and explore potential business scenarios.

## Hackathon

Developed as part of **AMD Hackathon 2026**.

## Problem

Small and medium-sized businesses may need to make important financial decisions such as hiring employees or expanding operations without having a clear view of how these decisions could affect their future cash flow.

## Solution

The Digital Financial Twin uses financial data and AI-based analysis to provide insights into cash-flow trends and simulate potential business scenarios.

## Key Features

* Cash-flow analysis
* Financial forecasting
* Business scenario simulation
* Data preprocessing and cleaning
* Financial risk analysis
* AI-based decision support
